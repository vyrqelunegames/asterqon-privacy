ASTERQON Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how ASTERQON (Android package: com.asterqon.voyage), provided by VYRQELUNE Games, handles information.

1. Information handled by the game

ASTERQON does not require a player account. The game does not send gameplay data or personal information to the developer's servers. It does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

The game stores progress and preferences locally on your device. These include completed missions, records, crystals, resources, owned spacecraft, custom ship designs, achievements, and language, sound, and control settings. This information is used to save your progress and provide game features.

The current game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location.

2. Save files and backups

You may export or import a JSON save file using the system file picker. The game accesses the file you select for this purpose; it does not automatically upload your saves to the developer.

If you choose a cloud storage provider when exporting or importing a save, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled in the application's configuration.

3. Purchases and Google Play

Purchasing and downloading the game through Google Play are handled by Google. The game itself does not collect or store payment card details. Google may process purchase, account, security, and diagnostic information under its own policies.

Google Privacy Policy

4. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve reported issues. Please do not send passwords or payment card information.

Support correspondence is processed through our email provider. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We retain it only for as long as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is cleared. You can remove the game's local data through Android Settings by selecting ASTERQON and clearing its storage, or by uninstalling the application.

The in-game campaign reset preserves the flight record. To remove all local game data, use Android's clear-storage option. Exported save files are separate and must be deleted from the location where you saved them.

The developer cannot remotely access or delete saves stored only on your device.

6. Security

Game data is stored in the application's local storage, protected by the device's operating system. Exported files are readable by anyone you share them with. Keep your device and backup files secure. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children’s privacy

The game's local-storage design applies to players of all ages. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the game. If a child has sent personal information to our support email, a parent or guardian may contact us to request its deletion.

8. This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts to it. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving GitHub Pages. This website hosting is separate from the game's local storage.
